Frequently Asked Questions

Start My Trip Request

What does PC Fantasy Travel help travelers book?

PC Fantasy Travel helps with custom vacations, cruises, all-inclusive resorts, group trips, event travel, family vacations, flights, hotels, rides, activities, and vacation package ideas.

Can PC Fantasy Travel help if I do not know where I want to go?

Yes. Submit your dates, budget, traveler count, preferred vibe, and must-have details so PC Fantasy Travel can help narrow down cruise, resort, city, group, or event travel options.

Does PC Fantasy Travel plan cruises?

Yes. Cruise planning can include Virgin Voyages, Carnival, Caribbean cruises, holiday cruises, family cruises, romantic cruises, group cruises, cabins, ports, budgets, deposits, and insurance reminders.

Can PC Fantasy Travel help with group trips?

Yes. Group trip support includes traveler details, room or cabin counts, dates, budgets, accessibility needs, payment timing, cruise or resort preferences, and destination ideas.

Does PC Fantasy Travel handle event travel?

Yes. Event travel support can include hotel options, timing, transportation needs, traveler count, budget, and location preferences for concerts, sports weekends, shows, and special events.

Are prices and availability guaranteed?

No. Travel prices, rooms, cabins, flights, inclusions, dates, and availability can change until booking is confirmed. Review final supplier details before paying.

Should travelers buy travel insurance?

Travel insurance is strongly recommended for cruises, resorts, flights, group trips, event travel, and custom vacations because it can help protect against certain covered disruptions, cancellations, medical needs, or travel delays.

Who is responsible for passports and visas?

Passport and visa requirements are the traveler's responsibility. PC Fantasy Travel can provide reminders and resources, but travelers should confirm current entry rules for their destination, citizenship, itinerary, and travel dates.

How should travel payments be made?

Travel payments should be made only through approved supplier, vendor, cruise line, resort, hotel, airline, or InteleTravel booking channels. Do not send travel payments through personal payment accounts.

How do I start planning a trip with PC Fantasy Travel?

Start with the Plan My Trip form and share destination ideas, dates, departure city, traveler count, budget, preferred travel style, package interest, and notes. You can also book a consultation from the contact page.
