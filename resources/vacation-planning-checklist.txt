PC Fantasy Travel Vacation Planning Checklist

Trip basics
- Destination ideas
- Departure city or airport
- Travel dates and flexibility
- Trip length
- Number of travelers
- Adults, children, and ages
- Rooms or cabins needed
- Special occasion

Budget and booking
- Total trip budget
- Budget per person
- Whether flights are included in the budget
- Deposit comfort range
- Payment plan interest, if available
- Ready-to-book timing

Preferences
- Cruise, resort, hotel, group trip, event travel, or custom package
- Preferred cruise line, resort, hotel style, or vendor
- Room or cabin preference
- Excursions and activities
- Dietary needs
- Mobility or accessibility needs
- Anything you do not want

Travel readiness
- Passport status for all travelers
- Visa requirement reminders
- Travel insurance interest
- Traveler names as shown on identification
- Emergency contact information

Important reminders
- Prices and availability are subject to change until booked.
- Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
- Passport and visa requirements are the traveler's responsibility, but guidance/resources can be provided.
- Payments should be made only through approved supplier, vendor, or InteleTravel booking channels.
